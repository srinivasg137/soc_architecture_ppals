Connecting the block to the system.

I am a Principal Engineer at Synopsys with over 17 years of experience in SoC design, subsystem architecture and high speed interfaces. My experience includes PCIe, UFS, UniPro, MIPI M-PHY, SATA, CPRI and Ethernet.

My work spans architecture and microarchitecture, RTL design, synthesis, low power design and verification, timing closure, logical equivalence checking and RTL to GDS implementation. That implementation experience shapes how I approach architectural decisions.

Why this site exists

SoC PPALS brings together practical design questions, worked examples and interactive experiments. Each article makes its assumptions visible and explains the conditions under which a decision is useful.

I have also served as an Engineering Manager and Design Lead, led and mentored ASIC engineers, and contributed to the MIPI M-PHY and UniPro specifications. I am exploring how AI assisted workflows can support design analysis and technical communication, with engineering review remaining essential.
