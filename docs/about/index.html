Connecting the block to the system.

I am a Principal Engineer at Synopsys with over 17 years of experience in SoC design and high speed interface subsystems. My core areas include PCIe, UFS, UniPro and MIPI M-PHY, with a focus on microarchitecture, RTL and subsystem integration.

My work connects architectural decisions with implementation: synthesis, low power design, timing closure, logical equivalence checking and RTL to GDS. I work with verification and physical design teams to resolve issues across these boundaries.

I have also served as an Engineering Manager and Design Lead, led and mentored ASIC engineers, and contributed to the MIPI M-PHY and UniPro specifications. These notes share practical engineering reasoning and the assumptions behind design choices.

Why this site exists

SoC PPALS brings together practical design questions, worked examples and interactive experiments. Each article makes its assumptions visible and explains the conditions under which a decision is useful.
